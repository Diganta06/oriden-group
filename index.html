About Us

Oriden Group is a diversified business group, founded in 2025 under the leadership of Digant Mondal. From the beginning, our goal has not been limited to business growth; rather, we are committed to creating lasting value through trust, innovation, excellence and responsible leadership, and to establishing Oriden Group as a strong, trusted and forward-looking business group by leveraging future opportunities.

Oriden Group is committed to donating 6% of its profits to philanthropic and social welfare initiatives, helping build a better and more sustainable future.
